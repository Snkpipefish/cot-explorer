What is a setup? A market observation that has passed our quality filter — labelled A or B by conviction. Read-only, not a trade order.
Setups (this tab) shows only the published, A/B-grade observations from cot-explorer's own scorer — filtered, conviction-checked, ready to read. Bedrock (tab V) exposes the full scoring engine: every instrument, every driver, every horizon, including unpublished candidates that didn't clear the publish threshold. Use Setups for "what looks tradable today," Bedrock for "why" and "what's nearly there."

Read-only observations. The score combines trend, positioning, macro, fundamentals, event-risk, and structure. Each row links back to the underlying market in Positions. No price targets are given — this is observation, not orders.

Bedrock — the full scoring engine. Every instrument scored across 6 driver families (financial) or 8 (agri), with each individual driver visible on click. Three status views: Published = signals that cleared their min_score_publish threshold (tradable). Near publish = signals at 70–99 % of their threshold — almost there, watch for the next refresh. All = the full deck including unpublished candidates. Use the Asset-Class filter to switch between financial (FX/metals/energy/equities/crypto) and agri (grains/softs).
