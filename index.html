Setups are read-only. The score combines trend, positioning, macro, fundamentals, event-risk, and structure. Each row links back to the underlying market in Positions. No price targets are given — this is observation, not orders.

V Bedrock · Setup Engine Live mirror of bedrock's signals — financial + agri scoring, asymmetric per direction × horizon —

Bedrock evaluates each instrument across six driver families (trend, positioning, macro, fundamental, risk, structure) using ~96 individual drivers configured per instrument. A signal is published only when its score clears that instrument's min_score_publish threshold. Click a row to inspect every driver that fired.
